## Calibration and command-line behavior

`Level.RimTurnSpeedDegrees` is calculated as:

`configured Flipper turn rate * Level.RimTurnSpeedCalibrationMultiplier (2.5) * RimTurnSpeedMultiplier`

The configured rates remain per-level values, preserving their relative differences. The calculation applies only through the existing rim-turn path; below-rim movement continues using the configured rate. The calibration is applied once, before the optional diagnostic multiplier.

- No `--rim-speed` argument: multiplier 1; normal candidate default is the former configured rate times 2.5.
- `--rim-speed 1`: identical to the no-argument default.
- `--rim-speed 0.4`: reproduces the former configured rim rate within floating-point tolerance.
- Other positive finite multipliers remain accepted, using invariant-culture parsing.

The 2.5 value is a **PROJECT DECISION / MANUAL CALIBRATION**, based on Neal's arcade comparison. It is not a literal multiplier established by the ROM listing. The ROM-backed evidence is the distinct top-of-tube Flipper processing and level-derived top acceleration.
